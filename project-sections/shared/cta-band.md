# CTA Band Component (shared)

Used at the bottom of every marketing page above the footer. Props: headline, body, primary button, secondary button, optional video.

Defaults:
- Headline: "Ready to grow every location?"
- Body: "Book a call. We will look at your numbers together and tell you exactly what we would do first."
- Primary: "Schedule a Call" to /schedule/
- Secondary: "Get a Free Audit" to /audit/

Design: full bleed, `--darkpanel`, centered, 120px padding desktop / 80px mobile. Optional looping video at 20 percent opacity behind a dark overlay.
