# Reference: Ignite Visibility /services/franchise-marketing-company/ (industry-page capture)

Captured 2026-08-25 for the Lane 3 industry-template brief. Desktop only, 18 frames. The page our `/industries/franchise/` has to beat for "franchise marketing agency" (their own sitemap analysis calls their franchise family our biggest URL-family gap, D4). Ignite is a content-volume SEO machine (1,510 URLs, 1,273 blog posts); this page shows what a rank-winning franchise page carries. Structure transfers; the skin and the stat soup do not.

## Section order (frames 01-18)

1. **Hero** (01): dark ground, photo right. A VISIBLE BREADCRUMB (Home / Services / Franchise Marketing Company) above the H1. ALL-CAPS H1 "Franchise Marketing Agency for Multi-Location Growth" (primary keyword + benefit). Answer paragraph names the BUYER PERSONAS: "Built for CMOs, local marketing teams, and franchise development leaders who need scalable execution with location-level reporting." One CTA + a reassurance microline ("We'll email qualified businesses within one business day").
2. **Award badge wall** (01-02): 4 third-party badges under an "highly-awarded" H2.
3. **Services grid** (03-05): 8 icon cards, each service re-angled to franchise (Franchise Email & Lifecycle, Local SEO for Franchise Locations, CRO, Content), every card stuffed with linked stat claims. Repeat CTA below.
4. **Named framework / pillars** (08): 4 "PILLAR" cards (Visibility, Credibility, Conversion, Profitability), each "POWERED BY" a named product suite. Proprietary-system framing: the method has a name and software behind it.
5. **Sub-vertical sectors** (08-09): "Franchise Sectors We Dominate": VERTICAL 01/02/03 cards (Home Services, Health/Fitness/Medical, QSR & Food) with named sub-trades per card.
6. **Reviews band** (12): aggregate ratings row (Google 4.7/117, Clutch 4.8/173+) then 6 quoted review cards with names, roles, source logos.
7. **Video strip + blog feed** (15): 3 YouTube embeds, then "Insights from our franchise marketing experts": 6 franchise-topic blog cards. The topic-cluster internal-linking engine made visible.
8. **Close**: proposal form + footer (16-18).

## What transfers to our industry template

- **Breadcrumb above the H1**: visible wayfinding that doubles as the BreadcrumbList schema made real, and an internal link to the hub. Ours becomes the mono meta row (INDUSTRIES / FRANCHISE), linked.
- **Personas named in the answer block**: the visitor self-identifies in the first paragraph (franchisor CMO vs franchisee group vs development lead). Directly usable; ours must do it in plain words.
- **Sub-verticals as a first-class section**: their "sectors" block is our sub-markets section, and it seeds every future T3 sub-vertical URL (HVAC, family law...) as on-page keywords first.
- **The named-system move, honestly available to us**: their pillars are "powered by" products; we genuinely have Obsidion and the 90-day plan. Our process section IS this move without invention.
- **Blog feed slot**: their strongest SEO weapon on-page. Ours is a deferred slot (T9 is wave 6): the template reserves a related-articles block, rendered only once posts exist.
- **Reviews with sources**: structure only; ours is the testimonial slot, hard-gated on real named testimonials.

## What we do NOT take

- **The stat soup**: nearly every paragraph carries a percentage ("lower CPL by 42%", "442% increase", "40% and 106%"). Half read as one-off bests presented as typical. Copy-rules bans unsourced numbers outright; our proof stays in gated slots until real.
- **ALL-CAPS everything**: H1, H2s, card titles, links. Our case rules (sentence-case headlines, caps only in mono eyebrows) are the opposite and read calmer.
- The badge wall (we have no earned badges; the slot stays empty per the footer rule), the phone number in the nav, the AI-suite product naming ("Amplify AI Suite": copy-rules bans AI-as-adjective for our services), and the two floating chat/accessibility widgets occluding every frame.
- The 8-card icon grid: enumeration by icon cards is exactly what our 6.10 ruled list replaces.
