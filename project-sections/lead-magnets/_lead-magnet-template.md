# Lead Magnet Page Template

Public, indexable. Goal: email capture, then the download, then a nudge to book a call.

## Page
- URL: /resources/[slug]/
- Title tag: `[PLACEHOLDER]`
- Meta description: `[PLACEHOLDER]`
- Asset: `[PLACEHOLDER: PDF, checklist, calculator, template]`

## Section order
1. Hero: eyebrow "FREE GUIDE" (or CHECKLIST, CALCULATOR), H1 with the promise, sub with what is inside, email form (name + email + number of locations) with button "Download the Guide".
2. Mockup image of the asset. `[PLACEHOLDER: design a cover]`
3. What is inside: 4 to 6 bullets.
4. Who it is for: one short paragraph.
5. Small proof block: one case study card or testimonial.
6. Second form (same fields).

## Thank-you
- Delivers the file instantly and by email.
- One line: "Want us to walk you through it? Schedule a Call."

## Tracking
- Lead event on submit. Tag the contact with the lead magnet slug in the CRM.
