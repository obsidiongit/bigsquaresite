## Design direction

- Light section, GridLines on. SectionHeader: eyebrow row, H2, sub in the right column.
- Three **process cards** across the grid (4 columns each), metacci-style: outlined only (1px `--sec-line`, radius 16, no fill), generous padding. Inside each, top to bottom:
  - Day-range **outline chip**: 12px mono uppercase, the numerals in `--sec-acc` ("DAYS 1 TO 10").
  - Phase title in Apfel 700 at `--text-h3` ("Get set up" / "Launch" / "Scale").
  - Checklist: four rows, check glyph in `--sec-acc`, text at `--text-small`.
- A single horizontal hairline runs behind the three chips, connecting the phases across the section. It draws in on entry (this is the old spec's "line draws in", now a rule not a decoration).
- Closing line centered below the cards in Apfel 700, then the two-CTA pair centered.

## Motion

- Connecting hairline: SeparatorIn (600ms) as the section enters.
- Cards Reveal left to right, 80ms stagger; checklist rows stagger 40ms inside each card.

## Mobile (375)

Cards stack vertically in order. The connecting hairline becomes a short vertical rule between stacked cards (or is dropped if it reads as clutter; note the choice in review). Chips and checklists unchanged. CTAs full width.
