## Decisions locked with Brad

v3 (concept session, 2026-08-25):

1. **The centrepiece is 90 squares, one per day.** The company is called BigSquare, so the progress bar is made of the brand mark. The grid is 10 across and 9 down, which maps rows to phases exactly: row 1 is days 1 to 10, rows 2 and 3 are days 11 to 30, rows 4 through 9 are days 31 to 90.
2. **The blue slab returns, behind the centrepiece only.** Same object as the featured work panel: brand blue fill, radius 32, a `RoughAnnotation` box squiggle stroked in the panel's own blue so the edge boils. Headline and sub stay on paper above it.
3. **Twelve marked milestone days.** Each checklist bullet pins to a specific day so scrubbing hits real beats. The day numbers are invented until confirmed; see "Open with Brad".
4. **The cube is not involved.** The companion's journey ends at the portal window (9.portal.md). The slab grows from a single day-cell-sized square at its own centre. Do not reach for `HomeCanvas`.

v3.1 (round 2, Brad's review of the built v3, 2026-08-25):

5. **Pinned runway.** v3's plain scroll map played the fill in ~1.5 viewports; Brad: "just happens way too fast... needs to lock and stop the scroll so you can actually scroll through it at a reasonable pace." The stage (headline + slab) pins sticky inside a 160svh runway on desktop and the whole choreography scrubs 1:1. This supersedes v3's no-pin decision and makes the section the page's THIRD pin, on Brad's explicit call. FeaturedWork's architecture (runway as sibling content, sticky offset clearing the 72px nav).
6. **Drag to scrub, hover only highlights.** v3's hover-scrub read as chaos on an accidental mouse-over. Hover now highlights the cell under the pointer (cursor-grab, an invitation); the clock moves only on press-drag, and it CHASES the drag target with a distance-scaled tween so far jumps cascade in day order instead of teleporting. Release eases back to the scroll-derived day.
7. **The merge is scroll-gated.** v3 merged when `day` hit 90 from any source, so dragging to the last cell whited the board out mid-play (Brad's screenshot). The 90-to-one-square merge now fires on its own pin beat, never from the day value.
8. **The finale: the merged square becomes the CTA.** v3's ending was a blank white square ("very unsatisfying"). Now a white panel grows out of the merged square across the slab, leaving a blue ring, and carries the closing ask (Youtech's "Ready to Get Started?" frame, translated): the closing line with a hand-drawn circle on "day 90", a Schedule a Call pill, the panel's own boiling white edge squiggle, and a hand-drawn smiley doodle. Brad's ask overrides the moodboard's playful-out rule for this surface: fun, fresh, hand-drawn.
9. **The readout column got rebuilt** ("the text on the right side is kind of weak"): a phase rail of three range chips with the active one inverted, a display-scale mono day numeral over "/ 90", the phase name at h3 in the display face, then the milestone rows.

## The choreography (desktop, pin progress 0..1 of the 160svh runway)

- **FILL [0.02, 0.60]:** the day clock runs 0 to 90, ~1svh of scroll per day. Cells fill left to right, top to bottom; the readout tracks.
- **MERGE at 0.65:** gap and cell radius animate to 0 (400ms swoop), the milestone punch-outs fade, 90 squares become one.
- **EXPAND [0.72, 0.88]:** the white finale panel grows out of the merged square's measured rect across the slab (clip-path, geometry measured not guessed), leaving a blue ring inside the slab's boiling edge. The panel wears its own white box squiggle.
- **[0.72, 1]:** finale content fades in; the circle, underline-grade inks, and smiley draw at >= 0.9 (`rearm`: they retract on scroll-back).
- A **checkpoint band** covers merge through finale (pin 0.62 to 1, `useScrollCheckpoints` bands, glide ~1600ms/vh) so an idle park can never sit half-merged. Fill beats stay free: drag-scrubbing is the point there.
- Everything is reversible on scroll-back.

## The interaction

- **Scroll owns the clock** (pin progress on desktop, a slab-approach map on mobile).
- **Drag scrubs.** Press and drag on the grid, mouse or touch, pointer-captured. The clock chases the target (distance-scaled tween, max ~550ms) so it cascades, never teleports. Release eases back to the scroll day over 450ms. Plain hover only highlights the cell under the pointer.
- **Keyboard.** The grid is `role="slider"`: arrows step a day, Page Up/Down jump phase stops (1, 11, 31, 90), Home/End, `aria-valuetext` "Day 37, Launch". Focus holds the clock; blur releases it.
- **Touch guard:** `touch-action: pan-y`; vertical swipes scroll the page, only horizontal drags scrub.

All three phases and twelve milestones render in the DOM at all times as an ordered list (sr-only on the motion path); the readout is the visual presentation, aria-hidden.

## Mobile (375)

No pin: the stage (grid + readout stacked inside the slab) is taller than the viewport, so a pin cannot hold it. The day clock maps to the slab's approach; when the scroll clock (never a drag) completes the fill, the merge and finale latch once and play forward. Grid stays 10 x 9 (~20px cells at 375, reads).

## Reduced motion

Settled end state, zero animating elements: no pin, no runway, slab open, grid merged and inert, finale panel visible with every ink drawn static, the full plan as a visible static list below the slab. Plain elements, never gated motion wrappers: framer never fires `animate` on elements mounted before the hydration-safe reduced flag flips (probed live in round 1; the trap and the branch pattern are in the component comments).
