# How It Works Section Specifications

Our version of Youtech's "Your First 90 Days." Strongest trust section on their page. Make ours at least as clear.

## Content

### Eyebrow
YOUR FIRST 90 DAYS

### Headline
"Here is what happens after you say yes."

### Sub
"No long onboarding. No guessing. A plan with dates."

### Three phases (timeline)

**Days 1 to 10: Get set up**
- Kickoff call with your team
- Full audit of your site, search, ads, and tracking
- Your Obsidion portal login
- Location list, budgets, and goals locked

**Days 11 to 30: Launch**
- First campaigns live (search, social, or both, based on your goals)
- Tracking fixed so every lead is counted
- Weekly check-in starts
- Creative round one delivered

**Days 31 to 90: Scale**
- Cut what is not working, put more into what is
- Landing page and ad tests every week
- Add channels as the numbers support it
- Location-by-location review

### Closing line
"By day 90 you will know exactly what every dollar did."

### Buttons
- Primary: "Schedule a Call"
- Secondary: "Get a Free Audit"
