## Design direction

- Tint section (`theme="tint"`), `py-section-y`. Content `relative z-10`. Width: EDGE; the lockup itself caps around `max-w-[72rem]` so the line lengths stay sane.
- The Youtech lockup (STYLE_GUIDE 6.4), one wide composition, no rails, no Nº:
  - Left: photo, `rounded-[16px]`, roughly a quarter of the width.
  - Middle: the quote set at `--text-h3` as the composition's heading, name + company below at `--text-small` in `--sec-mid`, then a "See the Results" link to the case study (quiet arrow link, not a mono rule row).
  - A 1px vertical hairline (`border-sec-line`).
  - Right: one oversized metric at `--text-metric` in `--sec-acc`, small mono uppercase label under it.
- Placeholder state renders honestly: photo slot is a `--surf` panel with a mono placeholder note; metric shows mono `[PLACEHOLDER]`, no CountUp.
