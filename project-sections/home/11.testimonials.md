## Design direction

- Tint section, GridLines on. No Nº label (proof-utility, the numbered narrative ended at 008).
- The lockup per STYLE_GUIDE 6.4, one wide composition on the 12-col grid:
  - Left: photo, radius 16, about 3 columns.
  - Middle: the quote set at `--text-h3` scale as the headline (it IS the heading of the composition), name + company below at `--text-small` in `--sec-mid`, then a "See the Results" rule link to the case study.
  - A 1px vertical hairline separates the right column.
  - Right: one oversized metric at `--text-metric` in `--sec-acc` with CountUp, small mono uppercase label under it.
- Carousel chrome: mono `1/2` counter (tabular nums, slash not a dash) plus quiet previous/next arrows below the lockup. No dots.

## Build with

Existing: `Section`, `Container`, `GridLines`, mono meta, `components/motion/CountUp.tsx`, shadcn `Carousel` (strip preset injections after `add`).

New this phase: `components/shared/TestimonialCard.tsx` (props per `../shared/testimonial-card.md`) + data array in `lib/`.

## Motion

- Slide change: crossfade 400ms, house ease. Auto-advance every 8s, pauses on hover and on focus within.
- Metric CountUp once per slide's first view.
- Reduced motion: no auto-advance, instant swap, final values.

## Mobile (375)

Stack: photo (smaller, top), quote, attribution, rule link, horizontal hairline, metric + label. Counter and arrows below. Swipe works.

## Theme, number, annotations

- Theme: `tint`. No Nº label. No annotations.
