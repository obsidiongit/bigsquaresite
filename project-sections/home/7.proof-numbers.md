## Design direction

- Dark section (`data-theme="dark"`). GridLines persist in `--linedark` (the rails recolor, they never disappear). Registration marks at the band's corners.
- Eyebrow row: `Nº005 / PROOF` (the Nº part in `--accondark`, label in `--ondarkmid`).
- H2 in `--ondark`, left. Optional one-line support in `--ondarkmid`, right column.
- Three borderless stat tiles on the panel, three-up on the 12-col grid (4 columns each): number at `--text-metric` (Bluu 700) in `--accondark` with `tabular-nums`, mono uppercase label below in `--ondarkmid`. Never raw `--acc` on dark (fails contrast, STYLE_GUIDE 1.3).
- Placeholders render honestly: the tile shows `[PLACEHOLDER]` styled as mono text, never a fake number.
- The band is full bleed; inner content aligns to the 1200 container.

## Build with

New this phase: `components/motion/SectionWipe.tsx` (STYLE_GUIDE 7.3: 4 to 6 grid-aligned column blocks rise `y: 100% to 0`, 600ms swoop, 40ms stagger, dark band entrance only) and a shared `MetricBlock` (number + label, reused by testimonials and case study pages). Data: `metrics` array in `lib/metrics.ts`.

## Motion

- SectionWipe plays once as the band enters (the dark ground arrives in stepped columns over the incoming section).
- Stat numbers CountUp (1.4s, house ease) once tiles are in view; placeholders do not count up.
- Reduced motion: band renders settled, values final, no wipe.

## Mobile (375)

Tiles stack single column, numbers at the 48px clamp floor. Wipe columns drop to 4. Band padding `--section-y`.
