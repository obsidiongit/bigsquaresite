## Design direction

- Dark section (`theme="dark"`, `anchor="proof"`), full bleed, `py-section-y-lg`. Content `relative z-10` (the cube passes over dark grounds too). Width: EDGE. No GridLines, no registration marks, no Nº label.
- Two-column composition (Youtech frame 05): left column headline in `--ondark` + support in `--ondarkmid`, then the metric grid (2x2, or 3-up row if the count stays 3). Right column: media panel, `rounded-[24px]`, reuse `FramedMediaPanel` or a plain darkpanel media slot.
- Stat tiles borderless: number at `--text-metric` (Bluu 700, tabular-nums) in `--accondark` (never raw `--acc` on dark), caption below in mono uppercase `--ondarkmid`. Plain-language captions per the Youtech ANALYSIS ("93% client retention rate" style: number restated in words).
- Placeholders render honestly: mono `[PLACEHOLDER]` text, never a fake number.
