## Design direction

- Light section, GridLines on. SectionHeader: eyebrow row + H2; right column support line or empty.
- Three cards across the 12-col grid (4 columns each; the 6-card future state wraps to two rows). Card per STYLE_GUIDE 6.4:
  - Full-bleed image, radius 24, bottom scrim for legibility.
  - Pinned on the image: solid mono chip with client name top-left, metric chip top-right (`+000% [METRIC]` format, value from spec data only).
  - Below the image: the outcome headline in Apfel 700 at `--text-h3`, then a "See the Results" rule link.
  - Hover: image scales 1.03 over 600ms; card lifts 2px with the soft shadow.
- Images are real work or brand-abstract placeholders clearly marked; never stock.
- "See All Results" is a secondary pill, centered under the grid.
