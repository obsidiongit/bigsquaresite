## Placement: inside the release beat

The section pulls itself up into that band (`-mt-[48svh]` mobile / `-mt-[72svh]` desktop, round 3: Brad flagged the headline landing mid-screen with a big gap above) so the display headline rises from below WHILE the film un-balloons into the cube, and the release rest state reads: headline in the upper third, cube floating on the RIGHT in the band between the support text and the first card row (round 3: at the reform's own end position, CARD_CENTER left, it clashed with the headline and cards; the heroEnd waypoint now sits right-of-center and the follower swims the cube over as the page parks). The overlap depth is bounded above: any deeper and the section's top edge climbs over the settled film panel before the reform starts. The hero's post-wrapper info bar is removed (nothing between the hero and the work).

Round 4 refinements (Brad, same day): the header (headline + support) is scroll-GATED on the canvas path: hidden while the film beats play, a quick load-in (baseline rise + support fade) once the section climbs clear (~46svh desktop / ~61svh mobile header top), re-arming on scroll-back so the text can never sit over the film (a sanctioned two-way exception to fire-once reveals; it exists purely to keep overlapped ink off the set piece). The reform itself now shrinks the panel down to the RIGHT: its shrink target slides from CARD_CENTER to REFORM_END (0.24, 0.03 desktop; HomeCanvas), where the cube forms at its companion settle scale, so there is no left-then-right dogleg and the heroEnd handoff is motionless. The settled panel margin grew to max(6vw, 96px) to clear the nav bar.

The pull-up only applies when the canvas hero runs. Reduced motion and no-WebGL heroes end settled (no empty tail), so the section falls back to normal `pt-section-y` rhythm. Order: Hero, Featured Work, Trust, Problem, Solution, Services.

## What this section does NOT use (Brad's calls, binding)

- No GridLines rails, no SeparatorIn, no registration marks.
- No Nº label and no bracketed indexes: the section is unnumbered (like trust); Problem stays Nº002. The only mono text is the card tag rows and the visible placeholder markers.
- Not the 1200px container: edges at `max(5vw, 40px)` (lusion's base padding).
