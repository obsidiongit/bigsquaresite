# Obsidion Portal Design Brief

v2, 2026-08-23. Replaces the pre-scrape spec's generic screenshot-left-features-right slide-in. Keeps the decisions.md layout (Youtech Youlytics structure) but rebuilt in the editorial system: framed preview panel + numbered rule rows. Build phase 2F, its own session: this is a flagship differentiator, give it room. STYLE_GUIDE.md governs.

Role: the transparency pitch. "Most agencies send a slide deck once a month. We give you a login." The section should feel like being handed the instrument itself.

## Content

### Eyebrow row
`Nº007 / PORTAL`

### Headline
"See every lead and every dollar. Live."

### Body
"Most agencies send a slide deck once a month. We give you a login. Obsidion shows every channel, every lead, and every call, for every location, the moment it happens."

### Five feature rows
1. **Every channel in one place**: "Google, Meta, search, email, and calls. One screen."
2. **Every location, side by side**: "Compare stores. Spot the winners. Fix the laggards."
3. **Every lead, traced to its source**: "Know which ad made the phone ring. Listen to the call."
4. **Budget pacing**: "See where every dollar is going before the month ends."
5. **Task log**: "See what we changed, when, and what happened after."

### Button
"Tour the Portal" (links to /services/obsidion-portal/)

## Design direction

- Tint section, `--section-y-lg` padding (the extra room the brief demands). GridLines on.
- SectionHeader: eyebrow row, H2, body in the right column at `--text-small` `--mid` (max 40ch).
- 12-col split below the header:
  - **Left, 7 columns**: the portal preview in the FramedMediaPanel treatment from the hero (radius 24, registration marks): a blurred Obsidion frame with a mono outline chip pinned top-left reading "PORTAL PREVIEW". Per decisions.md: clearly labeled placeholder, never a fake UI with fake numbers. Swappable for real screenshots or a screen recording when the portal ships.
  - **Right, 5 columns**: the five features as a **numbered ruled list** (same 6.10 component as the problem section): `[01]` to `[05]` mono in `--sec-acc`, a 16px lucide line icon, title Apfel 700 at 18px, one-liner in `--sec-mid` below. Hairline above each row.
- "Tour the Portal" primary pill under the rows, left-aligned to the rows' column.
- Deferred, on purpose: the scroll-linked set piece for this section (UI fragments assembling, or a scrubbed frame sequence per STYLE_GUIDE 7.4) waits until real Obsidion assets exist. Decision made with Brad 2026-08-23: the one scrubbed editorial moment stays unspent in v1. No Three.js tilt either.
