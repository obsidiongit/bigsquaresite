# Obsidion Portal Section Specifications

Our version of Youtech's Youlytics pitch. This is a major differentiator. Give it room.

## Content

### Eyebrow
THE OBSIDION PORTAL

### Headline
"See every lead and every dollar. Live."

### Body
"Most agencies send a slide deck once a month. We give you a login. Obsidion shows every channel, every lead, and every call, for every location, the moment it happens."

### Five feature blocks (icon, title, one line)
1. **Every channel in one place**: "Google, Meta, search, email, and calls. One screen."
2. **Every location, side by side**: "Compare stores. Spot the winners. Fix the laggards."
3. **Every lead, traced to its source**: "Know which ad made the phone ring. Listen to the call."
4. **Budget pacing**: "See where every dollar is going before the month ends."
5. **Task log**: "See what we changed, when, and what happened after."

### Button
"Tour the Portal" (links to /services/obsidion-portal/)
