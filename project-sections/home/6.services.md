Replaces v3 (three Youtech pillar cards), which was superseded UNBUILT the same day it was written: reviewing Youtech's own services region, Brad ruled the pillar-card layout "extremely boring. It works, but it's boring" and asked for a creative pass. Direction locked with Brad over a two-round option review this session:

## Design direction

- Light section (`theme="light"`, `anchor="services"`), `py-section-y`. Content `relative z-10`. Width: EDGE, no Container, no rails, no Nº, no bracketed indexes (v2's rejected look).
- Header row: headline at `--text-h1` left, support under it (max-w ~44ch, `--sec-mid`), the two Pills right-aligned (the two-CTA repeat pattern, same labels as Solution, deliberate).
- Grid below: `1fr` list + `~380 to 420px` panel, wide gap. Panel `md:sticky` under the nav.
- **List rows**: service name at `clamp(22px, 30px)` Apfel 500, arrow right. Hover/active: label and arrow to `--sec-acc`, arrow slides 4px, 250ms. The row driving the panel stays lit (affordance for "the panel is showing this"). Tap targets 44px+. Hairline between rows, mono eyebrow + hairline heading each cluster.
- **Panel**: hairline frame only (`border-sec-line rounded-[24px]`), NO fill: the canvas rides at z-5 under z-10 ink, so any opaque ground would hide the docked cube. Top: the DOCK BAY, an empty `[data-services-dock]` area (~clamp 240 to 320px tall) the cube parks in. Hairline. Below: the text block with a min-height so one-liner length never pumps the layout.
- Bay renders only when the cube exists (desktop + WebGL + motion). Reduced motion / no WebGL: the panel is the text block alone, fully functional.

## The dock (HomeCanvas + lib/services-dock.ts)

- Tracker measures `[data-services-dock]` per frame (the established sweep pattern: same rect math both sides, no shared state) and computes a dock blend from the services section's passage frac: engage ~[0.10, 0.24], release ~[0.78, 0.92].
- The companion's target lerps waypoint-journey -> bay center by the blend; scale eases to fit the bay (~0.46 of bay height). Idle bob and slow spin stay ON while docked (STYLE_GUIDE 7.4: alive at rest).
- **Spin flick**: each row hover/focus calls `flickCube(dir)` (dir from travel direction down/up the list), an impulse into a decaying angular velocity the canvas integrates. Clamped so rapid scrubbing cannot wind it up; decay ~3/s so it reads as flicking a well-oiled object.
- The journey's END retunes: the old services dissolve (frac 0.9) moves past the release, so the cube undocks, rises, shrinks, and dissolves over the section exit. Mobile journey unchanged (cube already exits at featured work; no dock, no bay).

## Motion

- Header BaselineReveal + Reveal; clusters Reveal stagger, rows stagger ~30ms within, once.
- Panel text swap: masked line-slide (old slides up and out, new slides up in, ~260ms, house ease). A continuous morph, never a bare fade-in appear. AnimatePresence popLayout; interruption-safe for fast scrubbing.
- No looping DOM motion (Solution widgets keep the region's loop budget). The live element here is the cube itself.
- Reduced motion: no cube (no canvas), panel swaps instant, hovers color-only.
