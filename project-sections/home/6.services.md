## Design direction

- Light section (`theme="light"`, `anchor="services"`), `py-section-y`. Content `relative z-10`. Width: EDGE, no Container, no rails, no Nº, no bracketed indexes.
- Header row: headline at `--text-h1` left with support under it (max-w-[40ch], `--sec-mid`); the two Pills right-aligned (this is the two-CTA repeat pattern from the Youtech ANALYSIS: same labels as the Solution section, deliberately).
- Cards: 3-up equal grid, `gap-6`, `bg-surf rounded-[24px] border border-sec-line p-6 md:p-8`. Inside each:
  - A small brand-abstract glyph chip: a 3x3 grid of 6px squares with 2 to 3 in `--acc` (LocationGrid heritage), unique arrangement per card. NOT a stock icon set.
  - Group name at `--text-h3` Apfel 700, linked to its /services/ anchor.
  - A hairline (`border-sec-line`).
  - The service links as quiet rows: label Apfel 500 16px, small arrow right. Hover: arrow slides 4px, label to `--sec-acc`, 250ms. Tap targets 44px.
- Card heights equalize; Organic's 6 rows set the height, shorter lists breathe (no filler rows).
- What makes this not-v2: no full-width mono tables, no directional fill rows, no bracketed indexes. The cards sit in the same family as the Solution widget cards next door, so the region reads as one system.
