# Problem Section Design Brief

v2, 2026-08-23. Replaces the pre-scrape two-column-with-icons layout. Direction locked with Brad: **statement headline + numbered ruled rows** (editorial, no icon grid). Build phase 2C (with the trust marquee). STYLE_GUIDE.md governs.

Role: the setup, not the pitch. One oversized claim, then four pain points filed like line items in a report. Calm, factual, a little severe.

## Content

### Headline (the page's one statement moment)
"Growing more than one location is a different problem."

### Body
"Most agencies are built for one business with one address. When you have 10, 50, or 500 locations, the cracks show fast."

### Four pain points (numbered rows)
1. "Locations get different results and nobody knows why."
2. "One vendor runs ads, another runs the site, and they never talk."
3. "Reports come late, and they hide more than they show."
4. "You get locked into a long contract before you see a result."

### Transition line (leads into the next section)
"That's what we fix."

## Design direction

- Light section, GridLines rails on. Eyebrow row: `Nº002 / PROBLEM` with a SeparatorIn rule above it.
- The headline is set at `--text-statement` (the single statement-size moment allowed per page, STYLE_GUIDE 3.2). BaselineReveal. Max width ~16ch so it breaks into two heavy lines.
- Body at `--text-lead` in `--mid`, max 56ch, under the headline.
- The four pain points use the **numbered ruled list** (new shared pattern, STYLE_GUIDE 6.10): each row spans the full container on a 1px top hairline. Left: `[01]` mono in `--sec-acc`, tabular nums. Right of the index: the pain point in Apfel 700 at `--text-h3` scale, one line on desktop. A closing hairline under row four.
- No icons, no x-glyphs, no cards. The rules and indexes carry the structure.
- Transition line "That's what we fix." sits right-aligned under the closing hairline, Apfel 700, `--ink`. Plain: the annotation budget is spent elsewhere.

## Motion

- SeparatorIn on the eyebrow rule, then BaselineReveal on the statement (700ms, house ease).
- Rows enter in order: each row's hairline draws (SeparatorIn 600ms), text Reveals with an 80ms stagger between rows.
- Transition line: plain Reveal, last.
- Reduced motion: everything settled instantly.

## Mobile (375)

Statement wraps to three lines at the 28px clamp floor. Rows keep the index + text lockup; text may wrap to two lines. Hairlines full width. Transition line stays right-aligned.
