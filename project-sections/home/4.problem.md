# Problem Section Specifications

Youtech's "most businesses struggle" block, rewritten for multi-location and franchise buyers.

## Content

### Headline
"Growing more than one location is a different problem."

### Body
"Most agencies are built for one business with one address. When you have 10, 50, or 500 locations, the cracks show fast."

### Four pain points (icon + one line each)
1. "Locations get different results and nobody knows why."
2. "One vendor runs ads, another runs the site, and they never talk."
3. "Reports come late, and they hide more than they show."
4. "You get locked into a long contract before you see a result."

### Transition line (leads into the next section)
"That's what we fix."

## Layout Reference
- Source URL: https://www.youtechagency.com/ ("Most businesses struggle to achieve successful marketing on their own" section)

## Components to Use
- Two-column on desktop: headline and body left, four pain points stacked right with small line icons. Stack on mobile.

## Animations / Effects
- Pain points fade-up with 80ms stagger on scroll entry.

## Design Instructions
- `--paper` background. Icons in `--mid`. Keep it calm. This is the setup, not the pitch.
