# Newsletter Capture Design Brief

v1, 2026-08-25 (Brad's ask, run in parallel with the portal set-piece session). A new homepage section, not in the original spec list: Brad sent the Youtech newsletter band and asked for the same composition directly after the Obsidion portal.

Position on the page: **directly after Portal**, before the testimonial. Build phase 2F2.

Role: the tertiary conversion goal from the project brief (join the email list). It is the quiet ask between two loud sections: the portal exhibit above it, the testimonial and closing CTA below. It must not compete with "Schedule a Call".

## Decisions locked with Brad, 2026-08-25

1. **Ground: light, our palette, NOT the reference's dark band.** Youtech runs this section full dark. Our dark budget is spent (STYLE_GUIDE 5: the proof band and the footer, and the closing CTA band is the one accent surface). Portal above is `tint`, so this section takes `light` and the alternation carries the rhythm instead of a third dark block.
2. **The image panel cycles placeholder frames.** No photography exists in the repo. Each frame renders as an honest placeholder naming the shot it wants, in the established convention (surf panel, mono `[PLACEHOLDER]` note, ghosted index numeral, the FeaturedWork treatment). Real photos are a data change in `lib/newsletter-frames.ts`, never a layout change.
3. **Keep the avatar cluster and the client count, with the real number: 600+.** Brad's figure, 2026-08-25: BigSquare has 600+ clients currently. This file is its source of record for copy-rules "Claims". The avatar discs themselves are placeholders until real client headshots are cleared.
4. **The offer is insights from the leadership team.** Same promise the reference makes. No cadence is claimed until Brad picks one.

## Content

### Headline
"Sleep soundly. You are in good company."

Single tone, `--sec-ink`, `--text-h2`, left aligned. The portal above already runs the two-tone split; repeating it here would read as a tic.

### Support
"Most marketing advice is written for one location. Ours is not. Subscribe to get what our leadership team is seeing across multi-location and franchise accounts, in plain words."

`--text-body` at `--sec-mid`, max 46ch.

### Form
One field, one button.
- Input: type email, required, placeholder "Enter your email address", visually hidden label "Email address". `--radius-card` (16), `--paper` face, 1px `--sec-line`, full column width.
- Button: primary pill, label **"Join the List"**. Deviation from the reference's "Subscribe": copy-rules requires 2 to 4 words that say what happens next, and bans one-word utility labels.
- Success state replaces the form with "You are on the list. Watch your inbox." plus a check glyph in `--sec-acc`. **It never navigates** (Brad, 2026-08-25): no thank-you page for a newsletter signup. The confirmation animates in where the form was and the wrapper locks to the form's measured height, so nothing below it moves at any width. Error state renders the server action's message under the field in `--destructive`.
- Posts through the site's single `submitForm` path with `formType: "newsletter"`, the page slug, and UTM params.

### Social proof row
Under the form: five overlapping 32px discs, then a `600+` mono chip in `--sec-acc`, then the line:

"Join the 600+ clients who trust BigSquare."

The discs are `--surf` with a 1px `--sec-line` and a 2px `--sec-bg` ring, empty. A mono `[PLACEHOLDER: client headshots]` note sits under the line until real, cleared photos exist. Number source: decision 3 above.

## The cycling panel

Right column on `lg`, below the form on smaller widths.

- **Square, not the reference's tilted portrait.** 1:1, `--radius-media`, 1px `--sec-line`, no rotation. The tilt is Youtech's playfulness and the moodboard rules playful out; a square is the brand mark's own shape and balances the copy column's height.
- **Registration marks** at its corners on `lg`+, our vocabulary's answer to the reference's logo sticker. The reference's sticker is also a white box around a logo, which the moodboard bans outright.
- **The counter chip** pinned inside the frame's bottom right: mono outline chip reading `01/04`, tabular nums. This is our translation of the reference's "SINCE 2012" tag: it earns its place by telling the visitor the panel is cycling, and we do not know a founding year.
- **Frames** come from `lib/newsletter-frames.ts`. Each is `{ id, note }` today and gains `src` when real photos land. The panel renders whatever the module holds, so the count, the order, and the swap to `next/image` never touch the section.
- **Cycle**: crossfade with a slow counter-scale, one frame every 4.5s. Pauses offscreen. Reduced motion renders frame 1 only, with the counter reading `01/04` and nothing moving.

## Mobile (375)

Single column, copy first: headline, support, input, full-width button, proof row, then the panel. Registration marks drop below `lg`. The panel keeps its 1:1 crop.

## Motion

- Headline: `BaselineReveal`.
- Support, form, proof row: `Reveal` stagger.
- Panel: `ClipReveal` on entry, then the cycle takes over.
- Reduced motion: everything settled, frame 1 static, zero animating elements.

## Steal from

- **Youtech**, the whole composition: `../reference-images/youtech-agency/youtech-home-desktop-10.png` (copy left with the form and proof cluster, framed image right). What we do not take: the dark ground, the tilt, the logo sticker, the white box, and the invented-feeling round number.

## Build with

Existing: `Section` (theme `light`), `EDGE`, `Reveal`/`RevealItem`, `BaselineReveal`, `ClipReveal`, `RegistrationMarks`, `useReducedMotionSafe`, `submitForm`, `getUtmParams`, the `.pill`/`.pill-primary` classes.

New this phase: `components/sections/home/Newsletter.tsx`, `NewsletterForm.tsx`, `NewsletterPanel.tsx`, and `lib/newsletter-frames.ts`. The form is the site's FIRST real input; its field styling is written to be extracted into a shared `Field` primitive in Phase 3 when the audit and contact forms need it.
