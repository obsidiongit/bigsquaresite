## Design direction

- 72px bar desktop, 64px mobile. Links `--ink` at all times.
- Over the page top: transparent background, no border. After 40px of scroll: `--paper` at 85% + 12px backdrop blur + 1px `--line` bottom border, 200ms transition. (The one place backdrop blur is allowed.)
- Mega menu: `--paper` panel, radius 24 (`--radius-popup`), soft floating shadow (the hover-tier shadow from 4.4). Column headers are 13px mono uppercase eyebrows in `--mid` (ties the menu into the mono meta system). Items Apfel 500 at 16px in `--ink` with a small lucide line icon left. Bottom strip is a full-width rule row in `--acc`.
- Dropdowns (Who We Are, Industries): same panel language, single column.
- Active link marker: a static hand-drawn underline in `--acc` (RoughAnnotation underline variant, fixed seed, drawn once, no boil, no draw-on animation in the bar). If it reads busy in build review, fall back to a plain 2px `--acc` underline and add a STYLE_GUIDE changelog note.
- The whole nav is a `data-cursor-quiet` zone (STYLE_GUIDE 7.6, matters when PixelTrail ships in Phase 3).

## Motion

- Scroll solidify: background, blur, and border animate 200ms with the house ease. No layout shift.
- Mega menu panel: fade + 8px slide down, 180ms house ease. Focus trapped, Escape closes.
- Link hover: underline slides in (plain CSS, 150ms). Pill hover per 6.1.
- Reduced motion: panel appears without slide, everything else is color-only.

## Mobile (375)

shadcn Sheet from the right: links stacked, Services and Industries as accordion groups, Login row with icon, "Schedule a Call" pill pinned to the sheet bottom. Sheet ground `--paper`, hairline dividers. The bar itself keeps logo + hamburger only; no pill in the 375 bar (it lives in the sheet).
