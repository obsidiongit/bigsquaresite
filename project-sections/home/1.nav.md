## Components to Use
- shadcn `NavigationMenu` as the base. Custom mega menu panel for Services.
- Mobile: shadcn `Sheet` sliding from the right with accordion groups for Services and Industries, Schedule a Call button pinned to the bottom of the sheet.

## Animations / Effects
- Transparent over the hero. After 40px of scroll: `--paper` background, 1px `--line` bottom border, backdrop blur 12px. Transition 200ms.
- Mega menu panel: fade + 8px slide down, 180ms. Panel is `--paper` with a 16px radius and a soft shadow, matching the Youtech look.
- Active link gets a 2px `--acc` underline.

## Design Instructions
- Height 72px desktop, 64px mobile.
- Over the hero video, links and wordmark are `--ondark`. After scroll they switch to `--ink`.
- Login is text plus icon, never a button. Only one button in the nav.
- Keep nav labels short. No icons next to top-level labels.
