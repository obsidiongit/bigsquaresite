## Components to Use

- `--darkpanel` overlay at 60 percent.
- Content left-aligned in the 1200px container, max width 640px for the text block.

## Animations / Effects
- Headline words fade-up with a 40ms stagger on load.
- Buttons fade in 200ms after the headline.
- Slow Ken Burns on the poster image if the video fails to load.
- Optional Three.js: a faint wireframe square grid drifting in the background behind the overlay. Skip if it costs more than 200KB or hurts LCP.

## Design Instructions
- Min height 88vh desktop, 100svh mobile.
- Text is `--ondark`. Eyebrow is mono, `--acc2` is too dark on video, so use `--acc` at 90 percent.
- H1 at 64px desktop / 40px mobile.
- Both buttons same height. Primary is filled `--acc`. Secondary is outlined `--ondark`.
- Everything above the fold must load in under 2.5s. Video loads after first paint.
