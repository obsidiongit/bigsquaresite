## Design direction

Light section (`data-theme="light"`), GridLines rails on. Two stacked moments:

1. **The statement.** Left-aligned in the 1200 container, hanging off the left rail. Top to bottom: a hairline rule, the mono eyebrow, H1 at `--text-h1` (Bluu 700) on two lines, subhead at `--text-lead` in `--mid` (max 56ch), CTA pair (primary pill + secondary outlined pill with directional fill), reassurance line at `--text-small` in `--mid`. The blue circle around "each one" is the page's signature annotation: `--acc`, fixed seed, gentle boil.
2. **The panel.** Full container width, inset from the viewport by the gutters. Rounded `--radius-media` (24), dark abstract placeholder loop inside (decisions.md: swappable file, one-line change when the commercial lands), poster image is the LCP element. Roughly 2.4:1 on desktop, 16:9 at mobile widths. "+" registration marks at the four corners where the panel meets the rails. Video: muted, autoplay, loop, `playsinline`, loaded after first paint; poster shows instantly.
3. **The info bar** closes the hero region (STYLE_GUIDE 6.6): "BigSquare Marketing" left, "[Results]" utility link, © year right, 13px mono on a 1px rule. No JS.

The nav sits transparent over this section with `--ink` links from the first frame (see 1.nav.md). There is no dark overlay, no white-on-video type, no min-height 88vh rule: those died with the v1 spec.

## Motion (mount choreography, not scroll)

Hero text animates on mount. Text and panel must both be settled inside 1 second; the LCP poster must never wait on JS.

- 0ms: ClipReveal starts on the panel (800ms, soft ease). The poster's center is painted from the first frame.
- 0ms: SeparatorIn draws the rule above the eyebrow (600ms).
- ~100ms: eyebrow assembles via TitleAssemble (the page's single allowed use).
- ~150ms: WordReveal on the H1 (700ms, keep the word stagger tight enough that the last word lands under 1s).
- ~400ms: Reveal fade-up on subhead, CTA pair, reassurance line (staggered 60ms).
- After the H1 reveal completes: the RoughAnnotation circle draws (2s, then 3-frame boil). It is ornament and blocks nothing; line wrappers flip to `overflow: visible` first (STYLE_GUIDE 7.3 contract).
- Optional, second iteration only: pear-style hero exit (lines drift up with per-line lag and 0 to 4px blur as the hero leaves). Cut it first if it costs any jank.

Reduced motion: everything renders settled, circle pre-drawn, no boil, video shows poster only until the user acts.

## Mobile (375)

Stack in the same order. H1 at the 40px clamp floor wraps to about three lines; the circle still lands on "each one" and may overflow the line box slightly (fine). CTAs full width, stacked. The primary CTA must be visible at 375x740 before the panel; the panel itself may sit below the fold. Panel goes 16:9. Info bar wraps to two rows if needed. No horizontal scroll.

## Theme, number, annotations

- Theme: `light`. Nº label: `Nº001 / INTRO` sits in the eyebrow row.
- Annotation budget used here: 1 of 3 (the circle). The other two live in the nav and the CTA band. Nothing else on this section is hand-drawn.
