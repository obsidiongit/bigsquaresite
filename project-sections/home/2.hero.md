## The set piece

A 320vh (mobile) / 480vh (desktop) wrapper drives a sticky 100svh stage. DOM scrub values are pure functions of native scroll progress; the canvas actors follow a TIME-DAMPED follower of that progress (rate 4.5/s), which throttles fast flicks into one legible motion without hijacking scroll. The canvas sits UNDER the text layer: the cube passes behind the exiting headline.

1. **p=0, the statement.** H1 at `--text-display` raised into the space beside the cube (flex spacers 1.15 above / 1 below, not bottom-anchored); words rise on mount (0.65s, house, 30ms stagger, done under 1s); circle draws after the H1 lands, then boils. Statement paragraph in its own clear row at the bottom right (never under the headline). Instrument strip on the fold. In the open area top-right floats THE SQUARE: a glass cube (transmission, ior 1.25, thickness 0.6, roughness 0.09, RoomEnvironment) with a SMOKE CORE inside: a single amorphous wisp, vertex-noise morphed and fbm-density shaded in the brand blues, drifting slowly, ghostly. At rest the cube drifts (ambient sine bob) and answers the pointer with a damped tilt. Desktop anchor (0.30, 0.24) of stage from center; mobile (0.24, 0.30), clear of the 5-line headline.
2. **p 0.08 to 0.46, the swoop.** The cube dips down across the stage behind the blurring, exiting headline (headline lines drift up with per-line lag and 0 to 4px blur through p 0.04 to 0.42), shedding a gentle fraction of a turn (max ~0.5 rad added on y), and settles face-on at the card anchor, lower left (mobile: lower center).
3. **p 0.38 to 0.48, the flatten.** The cube compresses into a thin glass pane exactly the size of the media card (16:9, 0.34 of stage width desktop, 0.62 mobile). The smoke shrinks away; the frost sheds (envMapIntensity and roughness ramp down) so it reads as a clearing pane, not a milky slab.
4. **p 0.44 to 0.72, the held card + develop.** The side text block slides in from the right (in 0.46 to 0.54, out 0.66 to 0.72) while the film surfaces ON TOP of the glass pane (renderOrder above it, slight z lead; gate 0.50 to 0.58), fading up inside the same footprint like frost clearing; the hidden glass is then retired. The film arrives as brand-ink duotone (`#0A2A73` to `#6E9BFF` by luminance) and develops to true color through p 0.50 to 0.70 (lusion's tint-to-color move). This is the page's lusion "card + paragraph" screen.
5. **p 0.62 to 0.92, the balloon.** The card grows out to the framed panel with the cloth morph: every vertex carries its own growth progress with the trailing corner lagging (uLag 0.34, normalized so all vertices land exactly at 1), plus a z arc toward the camera mid-growth. The sheet shears and curls like dragged cloth and resolves perfectly flat. Media counter-scales 1.16 to 1; corner radius holds at 24px (`--radius-media`) from card to panel.
6. **p 0.92 to 1, the hold.** The panel settles at 4vw margins on all sides (media as an object, lusion's settle: NOT full bleed), radius `--radius-media` 24. Mono film meta fades up inside the panel; registration marks outside its corners. Then the section releases and the info bar grounds the region back on paper.

Future direction (Brad, queued): the cube returns as a persistent companion that follows the visitor down the page (lusion runs one fixed full-viewport canvas for exactly this). Requires lifting the canvas out of the hero into a page-level fixed layer; do not build inside the hero stage.

## Engineering notes (learned in this build; keep)

- All DOM scrub bindings use callback-form `useTransform` with explicit clamps (framer-motion 13.1.1 range-array drift).
- r3f: NEVER pass a `uniforms` object to `<shaderMaterial>` as a JSX prop; the material ends up bound to a clone and useFrame mutations never reach the GPU. Build ShaderMaterials imperatively and attach with `<primitive object={mat} attach="material">`.
- three transmission: transparent materials are EXCLUDED from the transmission buffer. Anything that must be visible through the glass (the smoke) stays opaque (shrink it away, do not fade); anything that must cover the glass fades in above it (the film pane). Transmission + opacity fades do not composite reliably.
- The smoke core is "transparent" via stochastic coverage: an opaque ShaderMaterial whose fragment discards by fbm density against a spatial hash (a hand-rolled alphaHash). That keeps it in the opaque queue (visible through the glass) while the frosted glass blurs the grain into smoke. Its max noise-displaced radius must stay under the half-cube (scale 0.30 with the current displacement) or it pokes through a face and reads as a decal.
- Raw ShaderMaterials run in raw sRGB end to end: `NoColorSpace` on textures AND on token colors (`new THREE.Color().setHex(hex, THREE.NoColorSpace)`), `flat` on the Canvas. Otherwise the brand blues wash out.
- Budgets (STYLE_GUIDE 7.9): canvas lazy via next/dynamic ssr:false, frameloop "never" when the hero leaves the viewport (IntersectionObserver, video paused too), DPR watchdog (>34ms frames sustained steps DPR down 0.25 toward 1), everything disposed on unmount. LCP is the SSR headline. Measured 2026-08-23: the lazy 3D chunk (three 0.185 + r3f 9 + HeroCanvas) is ~232KB gz, over the 200KB written budget; three core alone accounts for most of it. Flagged to Brad; STYLE_GUIDE changelog carries the note. No WebGL → DOM fallback (simple rise into the framed panel, no cube).

Media contract (decisions.md): poster paints first (`public/media/hero-poster.jpg` as a texture; DOM `<Image>` in static/fallback paths); the loop mounts after hydration and cross-fades in once genuinely playing. Real 4K film = one-line change to `HERO_VIDEO` in `Hero.tsx`. Current placeholder loop: `public/media/hero-loop.mp4` (12s seamless ffmpeg breathe, 353KB).

## Reduced motion
