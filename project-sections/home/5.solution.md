Role: the answer to the problem section. One roof, one plan, one dashboard, shown as a composed set of panels instead of a template card row.

## Design direction

- Tint section (`data-theme="tint"`), GridLines on. SectionHeader: `Nº003 / SOLUTION` eyebrow row, H2, two-CTA pair in the right column.
- Bento on the 12-column grid, 24px gaps, every edge snapped (dropbox discipline). All panels radius 24 (`--radius-media`):
  - **Row 1, 8 columns**: lead panel on `--paper`. The body copy at `--text-lead`, plus a UI-fragment illustration: two or three abstract report fragments (a metric card, a channel row, a call log line) as bare rounded chips with soft shadows, floating in the panel's right half. Fragments are brand-abstract: blurred or `[PLACEHOLDER]`-shaped values only. decisions.md rule: never fake UI with fake numbers.
  - **Row 1, 4 columns**: the one dark panel (`data-theme="dark"` on the panel). "You See Everything" as H3 in `--ondark`, one-liner in `--ondarkmid`, a small blurred portal-frame motif with a mono "PORTAL PREVIEW" chip. Teases section Nº007.
  - **Row 2, 6 + 6 columns**: "No Long-Term Contracts" and "Built for Many Locations" on `--paper`. H3 + one-liner each. "Built for Many Locations" gets a small brand-abstract square-grid motif (rows of small squares, a few in `--acc`) suggesting locations rolling up. No stock imagery, no lucide icon grid.
- Bento budget per STYLE_GUIDE 6.4: one dark panel, zero accent panels (blue stays scarce until the CTA band).

## Build with

Existing: `Section`, `Container`, `GridLines`, `SectionHeader`, mono meta (chips), `components/motion/Reveal.tsx`, `components/ui/button.tsx`.

New this phase: `BentoPanel` (theme-scoped, radius 24, snaps to grid) and the UI-fragment chip treatment (reused by the portal section).

## Motion

- SectionHeader per its own pattern (SeparatorIn + BaselineReveal H2).
- Panels Reveal in reading order, 60ms stagger. Fragments inside the lead panel stagger in 60ms after their panel lands.
- Panel hover: none (panels are not links). Fragment chips are static, no looping motion (live-element budget).
- Reduced motion: settled.

## Mobile (375)

Panels stack full width in content order: lead, dark, contracts, locations. Fragments shrink but stay visible inside the lead panel. Gaps 16px.

## Theme, number, annotations

- Theme: `tint`; the dark panel is a panel-scoped `dark` theme, not a section theme.
- Nº label: `Nº003 / SOLUTION`. No annotations here.
