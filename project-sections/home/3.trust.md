## Components to Use
- Infinite horizontal marquee. Two duplicated tracks for a seamless loop. CSS animation, not JS, so it is cheap.
- Logos as SVG where possible, height-normalized to 32px.

## Animations / Effects
- Continuous scroll, 40s per loop, pauses on hover.
- Reduced motion: static centered grid instead.

## Design Instructions
- Sits directly under the hero on `--paper`. Padding 48px top and bottom, smaller than a normal section.
- Soft fade masks on the left and right edges.
- Never imply partner status we do not have. The label says "work with," not "certified by."
