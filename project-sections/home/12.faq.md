### Questions and answers (first drafts, keep answers under 60 words)

**Do you only work with franchises?**
"No. We work with any brand that has more than one location. Franchisors, franchisee groups, and regional brands with several offices."

**Do I have to sign a long contract?**
"No. We work month to month. We keep you by getting results, not by locking you in."

**Who owns the ad accounts and the website?**
"You do. Always. If you ever leave, everything stays with you."

**How fast will I see results?**
"Paid ads can produce leads in the first two weeks. Search takes longer, usually 3 to 6 months to move. We show you both in the portal from day one."

**Do you work with every location or just the brand?**
"Both. We can run the national brand, the local stores, or the whole thing together with location-level budgets and reports."

**Where are you located?**
"Denver, Colorado and Tampa, Florida. We work with brands across the country."
