# Copy Rules

Every word on the BigSquare site follows these rules. This file is authoritative. If a spec file and this file disagree, this file wins.

## Reading level
Write at a third to fifth grade reading level. Short words. Short sentences. One idea per sentence. This is not dumbing it down. Smart, busy people buy faster when the words are easy.

Test: paste the copy into a readability checker. Target Flesch Reading Ease of 70 or higher. Headlines should score higher than body.

## Voice
- Direct. Say the thing.
- Confident, not loud. We do not shout and we do not beg.
- Specific. A number beats an adjective every time.
- Human. Write like one smart person talking to another across a desk.

Sample lines from the brand sheet that fit the voice (direction, not locked):
- "Proof before promises."
- "Performance you can audit."
- "Ads, measured."
- "We don't guess. We report."

## Banned words and phrases
These are low-value words. They make business owners tune out or get annoyed. Do not use them in any user-facing copy.

- AI, artificial intelligence, AI-powered, AI-driven
- automation, automated, automate
- leverage, utilize, synergy, seamless, robust, cutting-edge, next-level, game-changing, revolutionary, innovative, holistic, turnkey, best-in-class, world-class, unlock, supercharge, elevate, empower, transform (as a verb for what we do), scalable (as a buzzword)
- "in today's fast-paced world" and anything like it
- "we pride ourselves," "passionate about," "dedicated to"
- Cute rhyming or slogan-style labels for service groups. Service groups are Organic Marketing, Paid Advertising, Design & Development. Period.

Exception, recorded 2026-08-24 (Brad's call, supersedes the earlier "name products instead" lock): copy may say "AI" when AI itself is the subject being talked about, as in the homepage search section ("Many people now use AI tools like ChatGPT..."). Pair it with a real product name where it helps. The ban still holds everywhere we describe our own services: no "AI-powered," "AI-driven," or vague "AI solutions."

When we need to describe what these tools actually do, describe the outcome in plain words:
- Not "AI-powered follow-up." Say "Every lead gets a text back in under a minute, day or night."
- Not "marketing automation." Say "Email and text that go out on a schedule you set."
- Not "AI chat." Say "A chat box that answers questions and books the appointment."

## Punctuation and format
- No em dashes. Ever. Use a period, a comma, or a colon instead.
- No semicolons in headlines or body copy.
- No exclamation points in body copy. One in a headline is allowed if it earns it.
- No ALL CAPS headers except small eyebrow labels (like "CASE STUDY" above a title).
- Numerals for all numbers: 5 locations, not five locations.
- Oxford comma on.
- Sentence case for headlines. Title Case only for nav labels and button labels.

## Patterns to avoid
- Double negatives. "Not uncommon" becomes "common."
- Rhetorical question stacks. One question max per section, and only if the next line answers it.
- Triads for their own sake. Three things in a row is fine only if there are actually three things.
- Starting sentences with "Imagine" or "Picture this."
- Hedging. "We believe we can help" becomes "We can help."
- Platform jargon unless the page is for a technical buyer. Say "cost per lead," not "CPL," the first time on any page.

## Claims
- Never invent a statistic, client name, review, award, badge, or partner status.
- Every number on the site traces back to a spec file or a real case study.
- Partner badges (Google Partner, Meta Business Partner, Microsoft Advertising) are NOT earned yet. Do not display them. The trust row uses tool and platform logos framed as "Some of the partners we work with." See `project-sections/home/3.trust.md`.
- If a claim cannot be backed up, wrap it as `[PLACEHOLDER: needs source]` and move on.

## Audience (added 2026-08-31)
Brad, 2026-08-29: "BigSquare is a full-stack marketing agency with strong creative. Franchise and multi-location is one big lane, not the whole road. Ecommerce, software brands, and single-location clients read as equals."

What that means for copy:
- Never write franchise or multi-location as the default reader. Titles, metas, heros, CTA bands, and FAQ answers read wide.
- Franchise and multi-location copy stays full strength where the page is about it: /industries/franchise/, the D4 wave-2 service slugs, location pickers, and clearly scoped sections inside wider pages.

## Buttons
Button labels say what happens next, in two to four words.
- Schedule a Call
- Get a Free Audit
- See the Results
- Download the Guide
- Book a Meeting
Never: Submit, Learn More, Click Here. "Get Started" is fine in the nav, not as a primary page button.

## Words we like
grow, open, revenue, locations, proof, numbers, report, dashboard, plan, team, results, own (as in "you own your accounts"), simple, clear, fast, measured.
