# Project Brief: BigSquare Marketing Website

## What is it?
The main website for BigSquare Marketing. BigSquare is a full-stack marketing agency with strong creative; franchise and multi-location is one big lane, not the whole road. The site has to do three jobs at once: rank for marketing searches (including the franchise and multi-location terms), send paid traffic to focused landing pages, and make a visitor trust us enough to book a call.

This is not a brochure. It is a sales system with a homepage, service pages, industry pages, location pages, lead magnets, VSL pages, and application funnels. It has to look and move like a Framer or Webflow showcase site, because premium brand and design work is one of the things we sell.

## Who is it for?
(Amended 2026-08-31 per Brad, 2026-08-29: these groups read as equals.)

Owners and marketing leaders who have budget, have been burned by agencies before, and are busy and skim.

- Multi-location businesses and franchise operations. A franchisor with 20 to 500 units. A franchisee group that owns 5 to 50 locations. A regional brand with several offices. They want to open more locations and make more money from the ones they already have.
- Ecommerce brands that want one team across their ads, search, site, and creative.
- Software brands that want the same.
- Single-location businesses that want to grow where they are.

Top industries: home services, legal, healthcare, franchise systems.

## What's the goal?
Primary: book a call (Schedule a Call).
Secondary: request a free audit.
Tertiary: download a lead magnet and join the email list.
Site-wide capture: none. (The exit-intent ad credit popup was retired 2026-08-31; capture happens on /audit/, /schedule/, the funnels, and the lead magnets.)

## Positioning in one line
BigSquare is the full-stack growth partner for brands that want proof. One team runs search, ads, sites, and creative, and you can check the numbers any day.

(Amended 2026-08-31. The old line, kept for the franchise lane: "We help you open more locations and get more revenue out of the ones you have.")

## How we win
- Proof before promises. Numbers, case studies, and a portal (Obsidion) the client can log into.
- One team, every channel. No silos, no handoffs between vendors.
- Premium brand and creative work done in house.
- Simple words. If a fifth grader cannot read it, rewrite it.

## What we are NOT
- Not a cheap overseas template shop.
- Not a 300-person agency doing slow, generic work.
- Not a tool company. We never lead with "AI" or "automation."

## Additional requirements
- Fully responsive, mobile first. Most paid traffic is mobile.
- Fast. Core Web Vitals green on every page.
- SEO built in from day one. See `seo-requirements.md`.
- Every page has one clear primary call to action above the fold.
- Framer-level motion: smooth, purposeful, never distracting. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Graphite palette (01) is the site default. Defined in `STYLE_GUIDE.md` (origin: the archived design moodboard).
- All copy follows `copy-rules.md`.
- Hosted on Vercel. Blog is MDX in the repo for v1.
- Built so partner badges and real case study numbers can be swapped in without a redesign.

## Offices
Denver, Colorado and Tampa, Florida. A third office west of Colorado is planned but not decided. Do not name it anywhere on the site.
