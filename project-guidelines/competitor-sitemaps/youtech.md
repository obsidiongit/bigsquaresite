## Takeaways for BigSquare
- Two CTAs repeated at least four times down the page. Same two buttons every time.
- Proprietary names (YouRank, Youlytics) make commodity services feel owned. Obsidion plays the same role for us.
- The 90-day timeline is the strongest trust section on the page. We should have our own.
- Built on WordPress with WPBakery. Ours will be faster and smoother by default.

## What we are missing (crawl vs our sitemap.md)

- **Case study volume.** /our-work/ holds 75 URLs. We plan 5 or 6 at /results/. Their grid model scales; ours must too (the component already supports growth).
- **Sub-vertical industry pages.** They have 9 sub-pages under /home-services/ (HVAC, roofing, plumbing, solar, fencing, lawn care, pool, restoration, electrical), 7 under /medical/, 8 under /law-firm-marketing/, plus roughly 30 one-off industry pages at the root (finance, real estate, gaming, non-profits, and more). We have 4 industry pages and no sub-verticals. Sub-verticals under /industries/ are the clearest expansion path after launch.
- **City pages beyond office locations.** They run /chicago-digital-marketing-agency/, /dallas-digital-marketing-agency/, /phoenix-digital-marketing-agency/, /naperville-digital-marketing-agency/, /scottsdale-digital-marketing-agency/, plus city SEO pages under /seo/. We have only /locations/denver/ and /locations/tampa/. Matches their office cities, so ours match Denver and Tampa first.
- **Interactive tools as lead magnets.** /facebook-roas-calculator/ and /google-roas-calculator/. Our 5 lead magnets are static downloads. A calculator is a future lead magnet format worth considering.
- **Job posting URLs.** Each opening is its own URL under /careers/. Our /careers/ is one page.
- **Service URL variants we lack.** /microsoft-advertising-bing/, /youtube/, /brand-mentions/, /photography/. None are in our v1 sitemap; note them for later, do not add now.
- **Where we can win.** Their blog is nearly empty. Our MDX blog at 2 posts per week out-publishes them from month one.
