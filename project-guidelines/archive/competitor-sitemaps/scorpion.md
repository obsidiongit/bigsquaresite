## Homepage section order (for reference)
1. Video hero, rotating headline word ("MAXIMIZE Your Wins / Growth / Brand / ..."), one CTA
2. Partner badges directly under the hero (Google Partner, Meta Business Partner, Microsoft Advertising)
3. Google reviews carousel
4. Big numbers (businesses helped, revenue, leads)
5. Award badges
6. Bundled product (RevenueMAX) with video
7. Three pillars (Technology, Advertising, Marketing) with video tiles
8. "Pick your industry" grid
9. Integrations graphic
10. Closing CTA
11. Footer (thin)
12. Exit popup: $1,000 ad credit with a deadline and one button

## Takeaways for BigSquare
- Industry pages are the SEO engine. Ours: franchise, home services, legal, healthcare. Sub-verticals later.
- The popup works because it has one offer, one deadline, one button, and a logo. Ours is specced in `project-sections/shared/popup-ad-credit.md`.
- Partner badges under the hero is the trust move. We do the logo marquee instead until badges are earned.
- Their footer is thin. Youtech's is better for SEO. We use Youtech's.
- Large type, short lines, lots of air. Keep that readability.
