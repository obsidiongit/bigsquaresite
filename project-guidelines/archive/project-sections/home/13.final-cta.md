# Final CTA Band Design Brief

v2, 2026-08-23. Replaces the pre-scrape dark-panel spec. This band is now the page's ONE full-accent surface (`data-theme="accent"`, STYLE_GUIDE 5), the e2vc accent-footer move translated. Build phase 2H (with FAQ), built directly as the shared CTABand component per `../shared/cta-band.md`.

Role: the closing brand moment, part one. The only place on the page where the logo blue goes full bleed. It sits directly above the dark footer; together they end every marketing page.

## Content

### Headline
"Ready to grow every location?"

### Body
"Book a call. We will look at your numbers together and tell you exactly what we would do first."

### Buttons
- Primary: "Schedule a Call" (links to /schedule/)
- Secondary: "Get a Free Audit" (links to /audit/), as the Bracket CTA

## Design direction

- Full bleed `--acc` surface, `data-theme="accent"`. Inner content centered in the 1200 container, `--section-y-lg` padding.
- GridLines persist in `--lineacc` (white at 28%): the rails run through the blue. Registration marks at the band corners in the same white.
- H2 in Bluu 700, white (`--onacc`). Body at `--text-lead`, white at 72% (`--sec-mid` on accent), max 44ch, centered.
- Primary button inverts on this ground per STYLE_GUIDE 6.1: white pill, `--acc` text.
- Secondary is the **Bracket CTA**: mono uppercase "GET A FREE AUDIT" wrapped in a rough-drawn white bracket (`--sec-acc` resolves to white here). The page's third and final annotation.
- No background video, no texture. The flat blue plus hairlines is the statement.
- The transparent logo mark may sit small in a corner (dropbox keystone move); optional, decide in build review.

## Motion

- H2 BaselineReveal, body + buttons Reveal (60ms stagger).
- Bracket draws on entry (1.2s), then gentle boil. One annotation per viewport holds: nothing else in this band is hand-drawn.
- Reduced motion: settled, bracket pre-drawn, no boil.

## Mobile (375)

Centered stack, buttons full width (primary above bracket CTA). Padding drops to the mobile end of `--section-y-lg`.

## Theme, number, annotations

- Theme: `accent` (the only one on the site). No Nº label.
- Annotation budget used here: 1 of 3 (the bracket). Budget now fully spent: hero circle, nav underline, this bracket.
