## Design direction

- Light section (`theme="light"`, `anchor="problem"`), `py-section-y`. Content wrapped `relative z-10` (HomeStage layering contract).
- Width: the open-region EDGE (`px-[max(5vw,40px)]` from `lib/layout.ts`). No Container, no GridLines, no SeparatorIn, no Nº label, no bracketed indexes.
- One soft panel: `bg-surf rounded-[24px]`, no border, generous padding (p-10 up to p-16 at desktop). Inside: heading + lead-in in the left third (heading at `--text-h2`, Apfel 700 or Bluu per build test; lead-in `--text-body` in `--sec-mid`), the four x-lines in a 2x2 grid across the right two thirds, top-aligned with the heading.
- X-glyphs: inline SVG circle-x, 20px, stroke in `--sec-mid`. NOT red (the Youtech ANALYSIS do-not-take list rules out traffic-light semantics; semantic color only if a future comparison moment earns it). Text Apfel 500 at `--text-body`, `--sec-ink`.
- The old v2 statement headline is retired; the page's statement-scale moment moves to the Solution headline next door (see 5.solution.md v3).
